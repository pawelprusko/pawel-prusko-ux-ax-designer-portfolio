Generative UI Analytics Engine

CASE STUDY 03 / High-Stakes Domain / Explainable AI (XAI) / Risk Governance
Impact: Automation Bias Mitigation

Enterprise Co-Pilot & HITL Decision System

Engineered Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) approval workflows for high-risk financial processes, balancing automation speed with human cognitive oversight through Explainable AI (XAI) feature attribution.

01. Discovery & Cognitive Risk

In high-stakes financial approvals, operators suffered from automation bias: either rubber-stamping AI recommendations blindly (high risk) or completely rejecting machine advice due to lack of transparency (low efficiency).

02. System Flow & Interaction Spec

Designed an adaptive confidence-scoring workflow. Predictions above 95% confidence streamline to 1-click verification; predictions below 70% or with high variance trigger mandatory Explainable AI (XAI) SHAP attribution panels and required human justification.

03. Logic Validation & Vibe Prototyping

Co-validated confidence threshold limits with ML engineers and risk underwriters. Simulated cognitive friction during edge-case overrides to ensure human operators actively review factor weights before authorizing transactions.
